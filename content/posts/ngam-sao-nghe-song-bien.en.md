Have you ever wanted to step away from the busyness of life and live like Robinson Crusoe on a deserted island with your best friends? We did exactly that — one night camping on the shore of Son Tra.

## Getting ready

You don't need much: a tent, a few tarps, flashlights, water and something to grill. The most important thing is picking a clear, moonless night so the stars shine their brightest.

- Tent and sleeping bags
- Flashlights and a power bank
- Snacks and water
- A bag to carry your trash home

## When night falls

As the city fades behind you, the waves become the only sound. We lay on the sand, watched the Milky Way stretch across the sky, and told each other stories we had never shared.

> Sometimes what you need isn't to go far, but to stop for a while.

## What stays with you

The next morning the sun rose over the sea. A short trip, but enough for everyone to carry a little calm into the new work week.
