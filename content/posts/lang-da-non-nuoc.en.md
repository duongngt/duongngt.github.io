Every year on the 16th day of the third lunar month, Non Nuoc stone carving village celebrates its founders' day. It is a rare moment when the sound of chisels fades, making room for processions and stories about the craft.

## Over 400 years of craft

Non Nuoc village sits at the foot of the Marble Mountains and has more than four centuries of history. From raw blocks of stone, artisans create Buddha statues, sacred animals and intricate ornaments.

## From stone to sculpture

A large statue can take months: choosing the stone, sketching, rough carving, detailing and finally polishing. Every step demands patience and hands that know the stone.

## What I learned

Design is a lot like stone carving: you remove what is unnecessary to reveal the beauty at the core. Every time I pass the village, I am reminded of that lesson.
