In Da Nang, Quan The Am Pagoda sits at the foot of Kim Son, one of the five peaks of the Marble Mountains. Few people know that right next to the pagoda is a cave carved by nature over millions of years.

## The entrance

The entrance is narrow — you have to bend down to get through. But a few steps later the space opens up, with stalactites hanging like stone curtains.

## The beauty of the stalactites

Under warm lights the rock walls show every color: earthy brown, moss green, ivory white. Locals say many formations resemble the Goddess of Mercy, elephants or turtles — depending on your imagination.

## Tips for visiting

- Wear shoes with good grip; the stone floor is slippery
- Bring a flashlight to see the details
- Go in the morning to avoid the crowds
- Keep quiet — this is a spiritual place

If you visit the Marble Mountains, don't just climb Thuy Son — save an hour for Kim Son and Quan The Am Cave.
