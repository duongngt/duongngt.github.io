Sunrise is the most beautiful moment of the day; it fills us with a sense of hope. In Da Nang, all you need is to wake up a little early to catch it.

## 5 a.m. at My Khe

While most of the city is still asleep, the beach is already alive: people exercising, fishermen pulling basket boats ashore, and people with cameras waiting for the sun.

## Better sunrise photos

- Arrive 20 minutes before sunrise
- Place the horizon on the lower third of the frame
- Shoot against the light for beautiful silhouettes
- Don't forget to watch with your eyes, not only through the lens

## A new day

Every sunrise is a reminder: whatever yesterday was, today is still a fresh page.
