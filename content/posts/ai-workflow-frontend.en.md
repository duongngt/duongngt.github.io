Over the past year AI has become a familiar "teammate" in my daily work. Here is what I have learned.

## Write prompts like work tickets

A good prompt carries enough context: the goal, the constraints, an example of what you expect. The clearer it is, the closer the result.

## What AI does well

- Scaffolding new components and pages
- Writing tests and documentation
- Explaining unfamiliar code
- Data transforms and repetitive refactors

## What still needs a human

Architecture decisions, user experience and ownership of product quality remain the developer's job. AI suggests — I decide.

## My workflow

1. Describe the requirement clearly
2. Let AI produce a first draft
3. Review, edit and test
4. Save prompts that work so I can reuse them

## Conclusion

AI lets me spend less time on repetition and more on what truly matters: the people using the product.
