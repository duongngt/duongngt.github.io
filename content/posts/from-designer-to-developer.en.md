I started my career in Photoshop and Illustrator, designing hundreds of banners for Japanese e-commerce sites. Then one day I wanted to turn designs into real products myself.

## Lesson 1: Details make quality

Japanese clients are demanding down to the pixel. That habit means I never skip spacing, alignment or hover states today.

## Lesson 2: Design for users

A beautiful banner nobody clicks is a failed banner. Interfaces are the same — beauty has to go hand in hand with usability.

## Lesson 3: Speak each other's language

Having been a designer, I understand what designers want. As a developer, I know what's feasible. That bridge helps the whole team move faster.

## Advice

If you are a designer who wants to code: start with HTML/CSS, rebuild your own designs, then move on to JavaScript and React. One small step at a time.
